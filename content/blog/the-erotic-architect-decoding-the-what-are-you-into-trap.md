## Front-load the bullshit first

Ask a gay man with ADHD what he is into, and watch the system overheat.

Watch the pause get too long. Watch the chest tighten. Watch the mind open twelve tabs at once. Watch the Body Dashboard light up with static instead of signal. The problem is rarely lack of desire. The problem is that desire has been routed through performance, masking, and The Auditor for so long that the man answering the question often gives you a résumé instead of a body-based truth.

That is the trap.

A lot of men think the erotic problem is ignorance. They assume they just need more experience, better language, hotter partners, more confidence, or the right label. But for many gay men with ADHD, the real problem starts earlier and deeper. They learned to survive by reading the room fast, adapting faster, and presenting the version of themselves most likely to keep the moment moving. That strategy may help them pass a job interview, charm a first date, or keep a hookup from drifting. It does not reliably help them feel pleasure.

**Rule 9: Front of the Bullshit, Reward the Hero.**

Use that structure here. Name the bullshit first. The bullshit in erotic life is not desire itself. The bullshit is the performance armor layered on top of desire. It is the F-150 Performance Armor that says be competent, be ready, be chill, be impressive, be masculine, be low-maintenance, be kinky enough, be sexual enough, but do not need too much and do not reveal confusion. It is masking. It is erotic over-functioning. It is the internalized Auditor scanning every move for evidence of awkwardness, neediness, inexperience, intensity, softness, or mismatch.

The hero comes later.

The hero is the authentic sexual self that can actually feel pleasure. The hero is not dramatic. The hero does not arrive with fireworks and a new personality. The hero shows up when a man can notice what his body has capacity for, name what increases safety, and design sex around what is actually true instead of what looks good on paper.

That shift marks the difference between Capacity Catfishing and the Erotic Architect.

Capacity Catfishing happens when a man advertises a version of himself his nervous system cannot sustainably support. He presents as endlessly available, highly regulated, spontaneous, dominant on command, sexually fluent, game for anything, and easy to please. Then the actual encounter arrives. His attention fragments. His body goes offline. He cannot track his yes, his no, his maybe, or his limit in real time. He either pushes through, fakes it, dissociates, or leaves feeling defective.

The Erotic Architect does something else.

He designs pleasure from actual somatic capacity.

He tracks his Spoons. He notices when the Smoke Detector of the Brain is already hot before the clothes even come off. He pays attention to whether the body feels open, braced, flooded, restless, numb, hungry, or overclocked. He names what kind of touch, pacing, structure, language, and environment will help him stay present. He stops treating erotic life like a performance review and starts treating it like Clinical Architecture.

That is not less sexy. That is the beginning of sex that can be felt.

## Why ADHD makes desire hard to name

Start with executive dysfunction at the point of performance.

A lot of men with ADHD can think brilliantly about sex from a distance. They can fantasize, scroll, plan, imagine, flirt, and build an erotic persona with real precision. Then the live moment arrives, and the signal drops. Russell Barkley's point of performance framework matters here. The struggle is not always knowing in theory. The struggle is accessing, sequencing, prioritizing, and communicating what is true while the body is activated in real time.

That is why "What are you into?" can feel less like an invitation and more like a spotlight.

The question sounds simple. The body reads it as a demand for immediate coherence. Now the man has to sort through sensation, memory, fantasy, shame, identity, masculine expectation, partner expectation, risk calculation, and self-presentation in a matter of seconds. For a nervous system already managing distractibility, novelty-seeking, rejection sensitivity, and a lifetime of being misread, that demand can produce static instead of clarity.

So he reaches for the fastest available survival architecture.

He gives the answer that sounds skilled. He gives the answer that sounds masculine. He gives the answer that sounds sexually evolved. He gives the answer that will not get him laughed at. He gives the answer that matches the profile he has already built.

That answer may not be false exactly. It is often just incomplete.

The body knows the difference.

When desire is real, there is usually some form of signal. Heat in the stomach. Pull in the chest. Relaxation in the jaw. Forward movement. Curiosity. A grounded yes. When desire is masked, the body often gives another set of cues. Buzzing urgency. Hollow performance energy. Tight diaphragm. Numb genitals. Sudden mental blankness. A vulnerability hangover before anything has even happened. That is the system preparing for management, not pleasure.

ADHD complicates this further because novelty can masquerade as desire, and urgency can masquerade as arousal. Fast dopamine does not always equal deep yes. Some men chase intensity because intensity cuts through under-stimulation. Some men agree to dynamics they cannot metabolize because the anticipation feels cleaner than the actual embodied experience. Some men use porn, camsex, or highly charged scenarios as a way to bypass the slower task of finding out what they can actually feel in a live relational setting.

None of that means the desire is fake. It means the channel is noisy.

## The F-150 Performance Armor

Use the F-150 metaphor plainly.

The F-150 Performance Armor is the sexual posture that says, I can haul all of this. I can carry the scene, carry your expectations, carry my own shame, carry the script, carry the stamina, carry the role, and carry the aftermath. It is a tactical build built for load-bearing. For some men, that posture looks hyper-top and unshakeable. For others, it looks endlessly game, endlessly agreeable, endlessly down. Different styling. Same engine problem.

The posture works until it does not.

It works for getting picked. It works for looking competent. It works for avoiding the humiliation of uncertainty. It works for staying ahead of judgment. It works for delaying the moment when a man has to admit he does not know what he wants until his body is already in the room.

Then the invoice comes due.

He cannot stay in his body. He starts performing reactions instead of having reactions. He loses erectile response, not because he is broken, but because the body is not available for pleasure while managing surveillance. He overconsents. He undercommunicates. He crashes afterward.

That crash matters.

A lot of men think the crash means the sex was wrong, the partner was wrong, or they are bad at sex. Sometimes the real issue is simpler and more exact. The body spent the entire encounter holding up armor. Performance ate the bandwidth that pleasure needed.

That is the front-loaded bullshit Rule 9 is pointing to. Name the dread first. Name the armor first. Name the metabolic cost first. Then build from there.

## Capacity Catfishing versus the Erotic Architect

Capacity Catfishing says present the fantasy first and hope the body catches up. The Erotic Architect says assess the body first and build the fantasy from there.

Capacity Catfishing says lead with persona. The Erotic Architect says lead with capacity. Capacity Catfishing says answer the question fast. The Erotic Architect says Master the Pause. Capacity Catfishing says tell him what sounds hot. The Erotic Architect says notice what the nervous system can actually hold. Capacity Catfishing says be the guy who can do it all. The Erotic Architect says become the Better Captain of your own Body Dashboard.

That distinction matters in queer sexual culture because a lot of men were taught that erotic fluency equals immediate certainty. Pick a role. Pick a label. Pick a script. Say it cleanly. Execute. But many men with ADHD do not have neat, linear access to desire. Their turn-ons are contextual. Their arousal may depend on pace, novelty, predictability, sensory load, trust, body energy, time of day, stimulant rebound, relationship clarity, and whether the nervous system feels watched.

That does not make desire less real. It makes design more necessary.

The Erotic Architect does not wait for some final, polished identity statement. He collects data. He notices patterns. He runs smaller experiments. He treats every erotic experience as information, not as a referendum on adequacy.

He asks different questions. What kind of pace keeps me in my body? What kind of communication lowers static? What touch brings me online? What sensory inputs tip me into overwhelm? What role feels authentic versus performative? What kind of structure helps me stay present? What makes The Auditor louder? What helps it stand down?

That is sexual intelligence with somatic teeth.

## The internal Auditor in erotic life

The Auditor is not interested in pleasure. It is interested in exposure management.

It scans for risk. It tracks how you sound, how you look, whether you are taking too long, whether your body is doing what it is supposed to do, whether your desires make sense, whether you are enough man, enough dom, enough sub, enough top, enough bottom, enough experienced, enough chill. It keeps a ledger. It mistakes vigilance for safety.

For queer men with ADHD, the Auditor often learned its job early. Get it right. Do not be too much. Do not be weird. Do not miss the cue. Do not need structure. Do not ask too many questions. Do not show confusion. Do not let anyone see how much effort it takes.

Bring that conditioning into erotic space and the result is predictable. The man starts monitoring himself instead of inhabiting himself. He goes spectator instead of participant. He starts biometric spectatoring in real time. Heart rate up. Breath shallow. Pelvic floor tight. Attention split between sensation and self-assessment. Pleasure cannot deepen because the system is too busy producing acceptable footage.

That is why front-loading the bullshit matters. Do not wait until halfway through sex to realize the Auditor is driving. Catch it earlier. Catch it in the chat. Catch it in the mirror. Catch it when you feel the compulsion to sound smoother than you feel. Catch it when you agree too fast. Catch it when your profile becomes a sales brochure for a man your weekday nervous system cannot support.

## Reclaim the Hero

The hero in this framework is not the loudest or hottest version of the self. The hero is the sexual self that can stay embodied enough to feel genuine pleasure, desire, and choice.

That hero needs conditions. Build those conditions first. Name the pace you need. Name the kind of check-in that helps. Name what is off the table when capacity is low. Name the sensory conditions that help you stay online. Name when you are more fantasy-forward than body-forward. Name when you need structure before spontaneity. Name when you want erotic play spaces that are negotiated, explicit, and built with mutual respect. Name when you want connection without overpromising range.

This is where the industrial aesthetic belongs clinically. Build the frame. Load-test the structure. Reinforce the joints. Stop decorating collapse.

A lot of men try to reclaim erotic authenticity by searching harder for the perfect label. Try a different move. Reclaim the hero by telling the truth about capacity before the encounter demands improvisation. Front-load the somatic and boundary bullshit first. Put the real constraints on the table. Tell the truth about your current bandwidth, your pacing, your sensory profile, your role uncertainty, your need for check-ins, your interest level, and your limits.

That honesty does not kill erotic charge. It removes counterfeit charge.

Counterfeit charge runs on anxiety, fantasy inflation, and persona maintenance. Real charge runs on safety, anticipation, clarity, and enough nervous system availability to feel what is happening.

That is the actual reward.

## What this looks like in practice

A man asks, "What are you into?"

Capacity Catfishing answers with a polished brand statement. The Erotic Architect answers with design information.

Maybe that sounds like this. *I like clear energy and slower build. I stay more present when there's direct communication and less guessing. I'm into intensity, but I need pacing and room to check in with my body. I can sound more game in text than I actually feel in person, so I'd rather build from what's real once we're together.*

Or this. *I'm still mapping some of this. I know I shut down when I feel watched or rushed. I do better with structure, directness, and enough time to settle. If the vibe is good, I can get very open. I just don't do my best work when I'm performing certainty.*

That is not weak. That is erotic leadership.

It is also far more likely to produce sex that does not end in confusion, shame, or a cold dread crash on the ride home.
